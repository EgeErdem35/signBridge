## 1. Information (what is this study?)
You are invited to a usability test to evaluate how useful and accurate our sign
language translation app is.

- **What you will do:** use the app for about [X minutes], then complete a short
  survey (SUS) and answer a few interview questions.
- **Participation is entirely voluntary.** Declining or stopping has no negative
  consequence.
- **You may withdraw at any time, without giving a reason;** recordings collected
  up to that point are then deleted (see §4).

## 2. What data will be collected?
During the test we will collect:
- **Video recording** (your face and hands while using the app),
- **Audio recording** (speech and interview answers),
- **App usage / performance data** (e.g. accuracy, latency, interaction logs),
- **Your survey and interview answers,**
- **Your hearing/disability status** (for eligibility).

Your audio/video recordings and disability status are **special-category personal
data** under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (**KVKK, Law No. 6698**) and,
correspondingly, GDPR Art. 9. Processing them therefore requires your **explicit
consent**.
