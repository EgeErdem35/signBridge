## Ethical, Health and Safety Issues

SignBridge-TR processes camera images and audio of people, may involve Deaf, hard-of-hearing and speech-impaired participants in its evaluation, and may be used in health and pharmacy conversations. This section describes the data flows, keeps dataset terms, participant consent, ethics status and conditional recordings apart, and lists the risks of wrong translation. [`docs/ETHICS.md`](../ETHICS.md) is the project's working ethics and data policy [G2]. Nothing in this section changes it.

### Data flows

Table H-1 follows the data-flow table in proposal Methods 6 and `docs/ETHICS.md` §4 [G1], [G2]. It describes the proposed application design, not a completed implementation or a network audit. Criteria 9 and 10 verify it later [G7].

*Table H-1 – Application data flows*

| Data | Produced | Processed | Leaves the device? | Stored by the application? | Retention | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raw camera video | User's device camera | On the device by MediaPipe (landmark extraction) | Never | No | Not stored | Required design (O8, criterion 9) |
| Microphone audio | User's device microphone | Speech-to-text; browser voice commands (voice commands belong to PX1, conditional on D-23) | Only if an external speech service is used. Browser speech recognition is server-based by default unless on-device processing is available | No | Not stored by the application. Provider processing, location and retention must be confirmed | **Undecided:** on-device or external speech-to-text (SB-36 open item 5). If external, the "not stored" statement must also hold for the provider |
| Landmark sequences | On the device, from camera frames | On the device (sign classification) | Only if server-assisted classification is chosen; then sent to the project back end, never raw video | No | Not stored under the proposed design | **Undecided:** whether landmarks are sent. Landmarks are not treated as anonymous |
| Glosses and Turkish text | Glosses: recognition model. Turkish text: gloss-to-Turkish conversion, speech-to-text output or typed input | Project back end, LLM and TTS | Yes, to the project back end for sentence generation, Turkish → gloss and speech output; to a local or external LLM/TTS depending on the deployment | Not defined | To be set before the evaluation | **Undecided:** local or external LLM/TTS; providers, logs, caches and retention |

Two further points are not covered by the data-flow table:

- **Quick phrases.** MF11 saves frequent sentences for reuse [G7]. Where these sentences are stored and for how long is not defined in Methods 6 or `docs/ETHICS.md`; to be added.
- **Research-session recordings** are separate from the application's live camera and microphone processing. **Team's current position:** usability sessions are not recorded, and no video or audio is stored. This follows the team's agreed privacy constraint (SB-36 open item 2) and the Department Head's guidance on an information form stating that camera image and audio are used during the session but not stored [G3]. `docs/ETHICS.md` still lists session recording as undecided until the advisor has reviewed it [G2]. The SB-9 consent drafts still contain audio and video recording options [G4]; they will be updated in the follow-up information and KVKK form issue (SB-36 open item 4) [G3].

### Dataset terms

AUTSL is used under its terms for academic, non-commercial use with citation [G1]. Access was arranged under SB-16 [G5]. The terms apply to processing the data, to sharing them, to derived landmarks and animations, and to showing sample frames in public reports or demos. Access does not by itself permit redistribution of source recordings, derived landmarks or animations [G2], [G5]. Landmarks are not redistributed unless the licence allows it (deliverable 5) [G1]. Before AUTSL sample frames are shown publicly, the licence terms are checked (SB-36 open item 7) [G3]. BosphorusSign22k is an alternative only, under a licence agreement with its developers [G1].

### Participant consent

Under the working policy, every evaluation participant gives written, explicit consent, collected only after ethics committee approval (O8) [G1], [G2]. The SB-9 consent drafts (Turkish and English, v1.3) contain separate participation, KVKK privacy notice and explicit consent sections. They are drafts for advisor and institutional review and are not ready for participant signatures [G4]. Under SB-36 open item 4, these drafts are to be adapted into an information and KVKK notice form (TR/EN) before the Spring 2027 usability test [G3].

Usability feedback follows the team's agreed privacy constraint: anonymous and non-identifying; no names, no stored video or audio, no disability status linked to a person and no combinable demographics. Participants are told not to include names or identifying details in free-text responses; any identifying detail that appears is removed before the response is stored, and free text is never linked to a person (SB-36 open item 2) [G3]. The final evaluation protocol and metrics are pending: task completion, difficulty, 1–5 satisfaction and a free comment are proposed only, and `docs/ETHICS.md` also lists SUS questionnaires and interviews as possible measures (SB-36 open item 3) [G2], [G3]. Scripted, fictional service scenarios with no real patient data are proposed but not yet agreed (D-12) [G10].

### Ethics status

`docs/ETHICS.md` is the project's working policy [G2]. It records no ethics committee application and no ethics committee decision, and it requires applicable ethics approval and informed consent before any participant test, including simulated pre-tests [G2].

On 11 November 2026, the Head of the Software Engineering Department gave verbal guidance, recorded in the SB-36 consultation record [G3]. It is verbal guidance, not an ethics committee decision, and has not been confirmed in writing. On usability testing it differs from `docs/ETHICS.md`: the guidance indicated that no ethics committee application is needed when volunteers only use the application with no experiment and no personal data collection, while `docs/ETHICS.md` still requires ethics approval first [G2], [G3]. This difference remains open until the advisor has reviewed the guidance (SB-36 open items 1 and 9). Until then, `docs/ETHICS.md` applies.

### Conditional supplementary recordings

No supplementary recording is planned [G1]. New sign video or audio recordings would be considered only if the coverage audit shows that AUTSL is insufficient [G1]. Any such recording, including recordings of team members, requires both:

- consultation with the Head of the Software Engineering Department before recording (SB-36 open item 6) [G3]; **and**
- the `docs/ETHICS.md` protocol requirements: advisor agreement, applicable ethics approval, informed consent and data-use arrangements [G2].

Supplementary recordings would be a separate collection with their own consent and data-use terms. Evaluation data are research data and are not used for training [G1], [G9]. This document does not authorise any collection.

### Misinterpretation and healthcare risks

SignBridge-TR is an assistive communication aid for short exchanges within a limited vocabulary. It is not a certified interpreter and not a medical or emergency tool. **The system's accuracy has not been validated for medical or emergency use.** All accuracy, comprehension and latency figures in the SoW (O1–O5) are targets, not results [G7], [G14]. Healthcare scenarios guide the vocabulary and interface; they do not establish clinical safety or replace professional interpretation [G14].

*Table H-2 – Misinterpretation risks and mitigations*

| Risk | Example | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrong or missed sign recognition | A sign by an unseen signer, in poor light or with a hand out of frame is misrecognised. The SB-15 test found mid-sign hand dropouts [G13] | Live gloss list (MF3); low confidence or poor framing prompts a retry (UC-3); confirm/edit before speech (MF5, criterion 12); live-webcam results reported separately from benchmark results (criterion 1) [G7] | In requirements. Showing a confidence indication to the user: no linked requirement yet – to be added |
| Vocabulary gaps in health scenarios | The AUTSL class list contains hospital, doctor, medicine and pharmacy, but not appointment, stomach or pain; one hour class does not cover numbers and times [G1] | Scenario coverage audit before the vocabulary is fixed [G1]; fingerspelling fallback with a clear message when an item cannot be produced (criterion 13) [G7] | Coverage audit pending |
| Wrong gloss ↔ Turkish conversion | A medication instruction such as "twice a day, after meals" (pharmacist persona) loses its meaning [G1] | Output limited to library glosses; rule layer checked against the Current TİD Dictionary; held-out test sets (O2, RQ2); numbers and times signed explicitly in the pharmacist happy path [G1] | Targets; not measured |
| Speech misrecognition | The hearing user's sentence is transcribed wrongly | Transcript shown for review; repeat, correct or type the sentence instead (UC-6); typed fallback when STT fails [G7], [G8] | In requirements |
| Avatar signing errors | Handshape, orientation or facial expression is wrong or unclear | Captions, replay and adjustable speed (MF9); reference-conformance check; comprehension tests with Deaf TİD users if they can be reached (O4, criterion 4) [G1], [G7] | Not measured; without suitable participants the O4 user measures are reported as not measured |
| Regional or individual TİD variation | A user signs a variant that differs from the training data or the avatar clip | Lexicon records sign variants; the regional variant policy is an open team decision [G24] | Not assessed |
| Use in emergencies or for clinical decisions | A user relies on the app when an interpreter is needed | Research-session outputs are not used for real healthcare, emergency or similarly consequential decisions [G4]. An in-app notice that the app is an assistive communication aid, not a certified interpreter or a medical or emergency tool: no linked requirement yet – to be added | Proposed |

### Health and safety of use

- **Camera and microphone.** The application asks for camera and microphone permission and shows a clear message and retry path when permission is denied (UC-2, UC-6); SB-41 includes permission-denial tests [G7], [G8]. Raw camera video is not stored or sent (criterion 9). An in-app indicator showing when the camera or microphone is active: no linked requirement yet – to be added. Browser-level indicators vary by platform and have not been checked.
- **Motion and animation comfort.** Avatar playback has replay and adjustable speed (MF9) [G7]. The WCAG 2.2 AA checks under criterion 11 include the success criteria on flashing content (2.3.1) and on pausing moving content (2.2.2) [G16]. No further motion-comfort requirement exists yet.
- **Physical strain and session load.** Signing in front of a camera and completing tasks can cause fatigue or discomfort. The consent drafts provide for breaks and stopping the session at any time [G4].
- **Accessibility of the app for Deaf users.** O6 and criterion 11 cover readable text, captions, keyboard and screen-reader use [G7]. Many Deaf adults read Turkish as a second language, so replies are shown in TİD through the avatar and not only as text [G1].

## Social, Legal and Political Effects

### Social effects

- **Access to communication.** SignBridge-TR aims to reduce a communication barrier in short service and everyday exchanges between TİD users and people who do not know TİD. The intended contribution aligns most directly with SDG 10 (target 10.2) and indirectly with SDG 4 (4.5), SDG 8 (8.5) and SDG 3 (3.8) [G1], [G14]. These are intended contributions, not measured improvements.
- **Over-reliance and interpreters.** The prototype supports short, routine exchanges in a limited vocabulary of isolated signs. Situations that need professional interpretation, such as clinical decisions, are outside its scope [G15]. There is a risk that users or institutions treat the app as a substitute for interpreter services; the product is positioned as a complement. Its effect on interpreter employment has not been assessed [G15].
- **Respect for TİD and Deaf community perspectives.** TİD has its own vocabulary and grammar and is not Turkish done with the hands [G1]. The gloss examples in the proposal are illustrative and linguistically unverified [G1]. Review by a TİD interpreter and early involvement of Deaf users are desirable but not yet confirmed [G1].
- **Digital divide.** The app needs a device with a camera, a microphone, a modern browser and a network connection for the back end and possibly external services. Phone performance has not been measured [G13], and offline support and cross-browser compatibility are unverified [G8]. People without such devices or connections cannot benefit.
- **Bias from limited signer diversity.** AUTSL has 43 signers; its challenge release splits them into 31 training, 6 validation and 6 test signers. BosphorusSign22k has 6 signers [G1]. The demographic composition of the signers (for example age, gender, region or handedness) is not documented in the repository – to be verified. The model may perform worse for under-represented signers. Signer-independent testing and separate live-webcam reporting reduce, but do not remove, this risk [G1], [G7].

### Legal effects

- **KVKK obligations.** Camera images, audio and participants' disability status are personal data; the proposal treats disability status as health data under KVKK [G1]. The data controller, legal bases and retention periods must be confirmed by the institution [G4]. Signed forms, participant identities, identifiable recordings, credentials and private correspondence are not stored in the public repository [G2], [G4], [G5].
- **Dataset and library licences.** Table S-2 lists the recorded terms and the licences still to be verified.
- **No commercial use.** AUTSL may be used only for academic, non-commercial purposes [G1], and the SIGN-HUB-derived pilot clips are under a non-commercial licence [G12]. The SB-36 verbal guidance is conditional on the project remaining academic and non-commercial; the team re-consults if that changes (SB-36 open item 8) [G3]. Commercial use would need new data or licences [G15].
- **Liability of an assistive tool.** The application is a research prototype and may produce incorrect results [G4]. It is not a certified interpreter or a medical or emergency tool. How liability would be allocated in a deployed product is outside the project sources – to be verified.
- **Intellectual property of the team's code.** The repository has no top-level licence file, so the licence for the team's code is not decided. Individual pilot files carry their own SPDX identifiers [G12]. The university's intellectual-property rules for senior projects are not in the project sources – to be verified.

### Political and policy effects

- **TİD in Turkish law.** Article 15 of Law No. 5378 on Persons with Disabilities provides for the creation of a Turkish Sign Language system to support the education and communication of people with hearing impairments [G1], [G17]. Since 2017, the Ministry of Family and Social Services has published the Current TİD Dictionary online, covering the 2,000 most frequent words [G1], [G20]. Whether Law No. 5378 constitutes formal recognition of TİD as an official language is to be verified; this SoW makes no such claim.
- **Accessibility policy context.** The project relates to the SDG targets above [G14]. Further national accessibility regulations (for example for public-sector digital services) are not in the project sources – to be verified.
- **Position of the project.** The project takes no political position. It does not advocate a policy change and does not aim to replace public interpreter services.
